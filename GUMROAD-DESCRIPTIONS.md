## Caregiver Handover Sheet

A large-print PDF for adult children, siblings, relatives, and helpers who share everyday care for an aging parent. Use it to pass along daily updates, weekly plans, appointments, rides, groceries, and simple household notes without relying on a long text thread.

Printable details: https://caring-for-aging-parents.pages.dev/shop/caregiver-handover-sheet/

This is a digital PDF. No physical product is mailed. Print at home or at a local print shop on US Letter paper. Please do not write passwords, PINs, Social Security numbers, or account numbers on the sheets.

## Who-to-Call Sheet

A large-print phone directory for the fridge or telephone table. Keep everyday family, neighbor, service, clinic, pharmacy, and helper numbers in clear boxes so visitors know who to call first.

Printable details: https://caring-for-aging-parents.pages.dev/shop/who-to-call-sheet/

This is a digital PDF. No physical product is mailed. Print at home or at a local print shop. Keep filled-in copies in a private place and share them only with people you trust.

## Where-Everything-Is Organizer

A plain-English household pointer guide for recording where keys, folders, bills, manuals, and household items are kept. It helps trusted family members find physical things without putting passwords or account numbers on paper.

Printable details: https://caring-for-aging-parents.pages.dev/shop/where-everything-is-organizer/

This is a digital PDF. No physical product is mailed. Never write passwords, PINs, Social Security numbers, bank details, or account numbers on the organizer.
